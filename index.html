OUR STORY

A place to slow down.

Tucked into the creative neighbourhood of Shahpur Jat, Solchemi is a café built around good coffee, thoughtful food and unhurried moments.

Whether you're meeting someone, working for the afternoon, discovering something new or simply looking for a quiet coffee, Solchemi is designed to give you a reason to stay.
